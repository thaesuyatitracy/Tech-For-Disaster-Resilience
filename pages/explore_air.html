Tracking the Storm

Cyclones bring strong winds and heavy rain to coastal areas. Weather-monitoring tech helps track storms and gives communities early warnings.

Doppler Weather Radar

Doppler radar uses radio waves to detect precipitation and observe storm movement.

  • Storm Detection: Identifies heavy rainfall.
  • Wind Monitoring: Observes rain and storms.
  • Weather Updates: Supports forecasting.

Weather Satellites

Weather satellites observe the Earth from space and capture images of clouds and storms.

  • Cloud Monitoring: Observes storm development.
  • Cyclone Tracking: Identifies location and movement.
  • Weather Observation: Covers large areas, including oceans.

Cyclone Forecasting

Meteorologists use satellite and radar data to estimate where a cyclone may travel.

  • Data Analysis: Combines multiple systems.
  • Path Prediction: Estimates cyclone direction.
  • Early Warnings: Shares updates with communities.

Everyday Tech You Can Use

When a cyclone is heading your way, information is your best protection. These tools can help you track the storm and get official warnings.

Moezala Weather App

Try: Moezala (Available on Google Play).

Use this app to check weather forecasts and severe weather warnings. It is one of the most reliable ways to get official updates for Myanmar.

Zoom Earth

Try: Zoom Earth (Web & App).

Open Zoom Earth and find the satellite weather map. Choose a view showing a tropical storm or cyclone over the ocean, preferably near the Bay of Bengal.

Home Preparation Checklist

Try: A simple checklist on your fridge.

Arrange a few emergency supplies together, such as drinking water, a flashlight, a first-aid kit, and a battery-powered radio. Take a clear photo so you know what you have.

Family Preparedness Activities

  • Secure the surroundings, tidy the yard, remove loose objects.
  • Weather-warning challenge, quiz each other on warning signs.
  • Protect important documents in waterproof bags.
  • Emergency backpack challenge, pack your kit together as a family.
