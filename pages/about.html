Quick Safety Tips

Small habits that save lives in Myanmar.

  • Save local emergency numbers in your phone.
  • Pack a small go-bag with water, snacks, power bank.
  • Agree on a family meeting point.
  • Follow official warnings only.
  • Know your nearest shelter.

About This Website

What is the Amethysts Website?

The Amethysts website is a student-built educational platform. We want to explain how modern technology helps reduce the risks of natural disasters in Myanmar. Our website covers four main hazards | Earth, Water, Wind, and Fire.

We show how tools like seismometers, IoT sensors, Doppler radar, and thermal satellites help save lives. Visitors can explore each element, watch short educational videos, learn preparedness habits, and test their knowledge with interactive quizzes. We designed everything for students, families, and community members who want to be safer.

Quick Facts

  • Built by 4 students from Mandalay
  • Covers 4 natural hazards
  • Includes short educational videos
  • Interactive quizzes per topic
  • Aligned with SDG 4, 11 & 13

Our Vision

We envision a future where every community in Myanmar has access to reliable technology and knowledge to prepare for, respond to, and recover from natural disasters safely.

Our Mission

  • Educate our community on how technology can be used to monitor natural hazards.
  • Show how monitoring systems help detect and track hazards early.
  • Explain how timely early warnings reach people when they need them most.
  • Empower community members to respond to warnings and stay safe.
  • Build awareness, preparedness, and safety through community education.
