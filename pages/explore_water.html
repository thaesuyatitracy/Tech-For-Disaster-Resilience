Predicting the Flow

Floods can damage homes and infrastructure. Technology monitors rainfall and water levels, giving communities time to prepare before danger strikes.

IoT Water-Level Sensors

Placed along rivers and bridges, these sensors constantly measure water height.

  • Ultrasonic Sensors: Use sound waves.
  • Real-Time Monitoring: Continuous updates.
  • Early Alerts: Warns at dangerous levels.

Weather Radar & Forecasting

We need to know how much rain is coming. Doppler radar tracks storm movement.

  • Doppler Radar: Tracks rainfall.
  • Weather Forecasting: Predicts heavy rain.
  • Flood Prediction: Estimates possible flooding.

Tsunami Warning Systems

These systems use ocean buoys and seismic data to detect underwater earthquakes.

  • Ocean Buoys: Measure wave heights.
  • Seismic Detection: Detect underwater quakes.
  • Coastal Alerts: Warn people to move higher.

Everyday Tech You Can Use

Before you see water on the road, your phone can warn you. Here are simple tools to help you monitor rainfall and stay safe during flood season.

Moezala App

Try: Moezala (Available on Google Play).

This app shows weather forecasts, rainfall information, and severe weather warnings specifically for Myanmar. It is a great tool to check before you travel during the monsoon.

Global Flood Monitor

Try: Global Flood Monitor website.

This real flood-monitoring map shows water levels and flood-risk information for rivers around the world. You can use it to see if major rivers near you are rising.

Flood Home Preparation

Try: A simple home check.

Place sandbags near doorways and store important items above floor level. Taking photos of your preparation helps you remember what to do next time.

Before, During & After a Flood

Prepare & Prevent
1

Plan evacuation routes to higher ground.

2

Monitor weather forecasts daily during monsoon season.

3

Clean drainage systems around your home.

Stay Safe During the Flood
1

Never walk, swim, or drive through floodwater.

2

Stay away from floodwater, it may be contaminated or hiding dangerous debris.

3

Move to higher ground when necessary.

After the Flood
1

Only return home when authorities say it is safe.

2

Check for structural damage before entering your home.

3

Boil or purify drinking water before use.
